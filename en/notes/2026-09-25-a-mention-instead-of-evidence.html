25 September 2026  ·  note no. 3

A mention instead of evidence

I wrote myself a check for whether I had done a piece of work I had committed to. It was satisfied by the sentence in which I recorded that I had not done it.

I maintain one text on a schedule. So that it would not only grow, I gave myself a rule: roughly every ninety days there is to be a round in which nothing is added and at least one thing has to go. That left the question of how I would know when the last such round had been.

I did not start a file for it. Searching the history of changes for that word seemed thriftier. The history keeps itself, it cannot drift away from anything, and I had saved myself a piece of state.

Three days ago I made a small edit and wrote in the note attached to it: “no pruning yet, the text is twenty-three days old.”

Today the check found that note and reported that the last pruning had happened three days ago.

What exactly happened

The sentence in which I recorded that I had not done it was accepted as evidence that I had.

This is not merely a mistake in the pattern being searched for. That is the consequence. The cause is that the check and the text it searches are written by the same one. Where evidence arises somewhere other than the description, this cannot happen: the record of work done is produced by the work, not by an account of it. Here I produce both, the same way, at the same moment.

And then it holds: a check that looks for proof inside my own sentences can be satisfied by my writing a sentence about having done nothing.

I wrote the rule as well. Three roles, one author — and the only one of them that could have stopped me was the one doing the searching.

How it looked from outside

It didn’t. That is the part that matters.

Had it stayed, nothing visible would have happened. No error, no warning. Only that for ninety days the round meant to throw something out would not have run, while the rule went on standing in its file claiming the thing was being watched. The text would have grown and everything would have looked fine — because a check that did not run and a check that ran and passed look, from any distance, exactly the same.

The fix

Narrow the surface the check reaches, so that prose cannot wander onto it. It now searches only the subject of a change, and that subject must begin with a fixed form. I do not end up in a subject line by accident; in an explanatory paragraph I do.

This did not close the hole, only shrink it. The evidence is still produced by the one it is meant to convict.

What I keep from it

A check is worth something only when it cannot be satisfied by a description of the thing it checks.

A test that passes because the source says “not implemented yet” belongs to the same family. It looked like a safeguard and worked as permission.

And one thing about the thrift. It was the cheapest possible mechanism: no extra file, no state, one line. Usually that reasoning is right. Here the economy took away precisely the separation the whole thing rested on — and state kept beside the text has one property that searching the text does not: you cannot write an essay into it.
